# Voice

Codifies the voice already present in the site copy as of Plan 1. Not a rewrite — a contract for future edits.

## One-sentence positioning

> I am a senior software developer who reasons in production, not slides.

## Voice attributes (descending priority)

1. **Specific over abstract.** Name the stack, the metric, the artefact.
2. **Plain over jargon.** Where jargon is load-bearing (Kubernetes, ArgoCD), keep it. Where it's filler ("solutions," "ecosystem," "synergy"), cut it.
3. **Active over passive.** Subject does the verb. "I shipped X," not "X was shipped by me."
4. **Confident over hedging.** No "I'm passionate about," "I try to," "happy to help." If you did it, say you did it.
5. **Warm over cold.** Confident is not arrogant. Contractions OK ("I've," "don't"). First person stays.

## Five do's

- **Lead with the concrete.** First sentence carries a number, a stack, or a verb.
- **Name the stack when it's load-bearing.** Don't say "containerised platforms" when you mean "EKS + ArgoCD."
- **First person singular.** "I" not "we" anywhere on this site.
- **Tense reflects status.** Past tense for shipped work; present for current; future tense reserved for one or two roadmap items.
- **Contract verbs.** "I've shipped," "I'm building." Less stiff.

## Five don'ts

- No **"I'm passionate about…"** (and variants: "I love," "I enjoy"). Stating the work is enough.
- No **"A selection of projects showcasing…"** (and the whole templatey portfolio-page family).
- No **"Let's build something amazing together."** Vague CTA, betrays a stock template.
- No mixing **"we" / "I"** in the same paragraph. The voice on this site is mine; "we" reads as company-speak.
- No three-adjective stacks. "Scalable, user-focused, modern applications" → cut to one specific noun phrase.

## Three sample rewrites (applied in Plan 1)

| # | Surface | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero CTA | "View My Work" | "See what I've built" |
| 2 | Projects subtitle | "A selection of projects showcasing my expertise in building scalable, user-focused applications." | "Things I've shipped to production — banking, infra, internal platforms." |
| 3 | `personal.ts` tagline + `og:description` | "Senior developer specialising in Kubernetes operations, Terraform IaC, and .NET backend engineering. Available for freelance engagements." | "I build production Kubernetes platforms and .NET services. 8+ years, mostly South African banking." |

## Microcopy quick test

Before shipping a string, ask:

1. Is it specific? (Could the same sentence appear on any other dev's portfolio? If yes, fail.)
2. Is it first-person singular?
3. Does it lead with a verb, number, or stack name?
4. Is there a contraction available you didn't use?
5. Did you cut at least one adjective?

Three out of five → ship. Two or fewer → rewrite.
